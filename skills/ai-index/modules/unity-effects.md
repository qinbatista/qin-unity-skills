# Unity Effects

The [Effects entry](../../../unity-effects-skill/SKILL.md) selects reuse or creation. Use YoFaAssets' catalog and `effect-id` to bind linked reference assets. [Staged effects](../../../unity-effects-skill/references/staged-effects.md) owns formation and lifecycle. [Rendering and verification](../../../unity-effects-skill/references/rendering-and-verification.md) owns renderer binding, material lifetime and native checks. Linked games apply YoFaAssets' full profile to their own effect packages.

For a straight cut or occluder outline on a free material surface, use [edge diagnosis and native comparison](../../../unity-effects-skill/references/rendering-and-verification.md#trace-an-unexplained-straight-edge) and [surface reconstruction](../../../unity-effects-skill/references/staged-effects.md#continue-a-free-surface-through-occlusion).

For a reproduction claim, follow [the declared input and output boundary](../../../unity-effects-skill/references/graph-and-shader.md#rebuild-from-declared-inputs). Record the available authoring, fresh import, native motion and transfer evidence for each requested example.

For independent objects inside moving artwork, trace each [motion job](../../../unity-effects-skill/references/staged-effects.md#decompose-every-visible-object) through its [VFX Graph and Shader Graph nodes](../../../unity-effects-skill/references/graph-and-shader.md#author-the-live-graphs) to its [registered native measurement and freeze control](../../../unity-effects-skill/references/rendering-and-verification.md#verify-local-motion-per-object). Check the renderer target and isolated visible footprint of each draw in both the preview and its real owner. Preserve neighboring contact times in local controls. Use lossless frames and native coverage for dim signals and inspect the delivered animation separately. Test standalone actor entry and the persistent game actor.

The [fluid mixer](../../../unity-effects-skill/references/fluid-mixer.md) owns the editable public GPU solver, graphs, seven starting presets, 2D attack and waterfall applications, installer and two-receiver replay. Imported game variants and their cameras stay with the game. The [test map](../../../.agents/skills/testing-skill/SKILL.md) covers installer conflicts and native regeneration. The [reference replay results](../../../examples/fluid-mixer/Verification.json) record the verified release, camera comparisons and GPU controls.

The [2D application replay](../../../unity-effects-skill/references/fluid-mixer.md#2d-application-replays) owns the hit/closure and sustained-inlet checks. The [application evidence](../../../examples/fluid-mixer/Applications.json) binds the native films to both receivers.

The [side-view contract](../../../unity-effects-skill/references/fluid-mixer.md#side-view-contract) binds the rendered body, spray, contact silhouette and waterline to XY. The [side-view evidence](../../../examples/fluid-mixer/SideView.json) covers the changed projection, matched solver state and two native receivers.
