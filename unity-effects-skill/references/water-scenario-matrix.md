## Author a named case

1. Install the editable template. Pass `--upgrade` for an intact copy of the previous bundled release at the same destination. The installer checks its contents before swapping it, so existing game-owned variants retain their GUID bindings. If the receiver contains artist edits, preserve them and merge the template changes deliberately.
2. Generate a named prefab with the matching profile and parameters. The composer writes a game-owned prefab. Parcel variants also own a recipe. Place the prefab in the receiving camera and wire its source, contact or gate to the corresponding gameplay event.
3. Choose the matched physical control in the family guide. Capture the same camera, seed and time on both runs. Change the gate, gravity, collision, current, input charge or impact transfer. Compare the state and visible silhouette together.
4. Recreate the affected family in two independent projects. The replay checks exact field and camera outputs, writes the per-case measurements to `FluidVerification.json` and exports each uncut take to `Subjects/`. Open `Review.html` to play a case or inspect its matched control. When the recorded camera comparison cannot resolve a weak inlet impact, the review displays both native GPU water-height grids at the strongest measured response and labels their shared colour scale.

From the installed Effects Skill, use the receiving project's registered native launcher if one exists. Use `py -3 -B` in Windows PowerShell.

```sh
python3 -B scripts/fluid_mixer.py --project "/path/to/Game" --destination Assets/Effects/Water
python3 -B scripts/compose_water.py --project "/path/to/Game" --template Assets/Effects/Water --name HarborWake --profile boat-wake --drive .9 --wave-speed 1.4
python3 -B scripts/compose_water.py --project "/path/to/Game" --template Assets/Effects/Water --name CliffDrop --profile drip --count 1 --span 0
python3 -B scripts/replay_fluids.py --editor "/path/to/Unity" --launcher "/path/to/registered/unity_cli_daily.py" --work "/path/to/task/Cache/temp-run/replay" --case all --preview --video
python3 -B scripts/sweep_water.py --work "/path/to/task/Cache/temp-run/authoring"
```

## Interpret the checks

The [authoring sweep](../scripts/sweep_water.py) enumerates the Cartesian product of inherited, minimum and maximum values exposed for each profile. It invokes the composer, reopens each valid prefab and checks its fields and recipe binding. It also exercises incompatible durations, out-of-range inputs, nonfinite numbers, unsupported settings and fractional drop counts. A matrix cell in the authoring receipt represents serialized assets. The 37 filmed subjects have separate simulation, image and clock evidence. The full run also executes the [repeated-cast resource comparison](fluid-mixer.md#verify-resource-reuse-across-casts) once per receiver.

Keep inlet duration within cast duration. A curtain needs its authored hold plus 1.40 s for closure and runoff. A zero-gravity mass is valid. A dam profile accepts up to 0.85 m of head above its upstream depth. For a new slope, obstacle layout, camera backend or spell event, add the physical input, a matched control and a camera receipt for that case. Use the [performance review](../../unity-performance-skill/SKILL.md#review-every-unity-task) when increasing grid size, capture frequency or simultaneous casts.

## Map a new brief to the existing boundaries

| Game brief | Physical setup to try first |
| --- | --- |
| Leaking roof, broken pipe outlet, cave drip or rainstorm | Point or distributed falling inlet. Set drop count, source width and gravity, then observe contact with the receiving pool. |
| Nozzle, irrigation jet, fountain or geyser | Finite ballistic inlet. Set the launch direction and source schedule. Place a solid receiver if the jet can hit a ledge or shield. |
| Waterfall or sluice stopped by a valve | Continuous sheet plus receiving pool. Compare two valve times and follow the tail after cutoff. |
| Sudden flood on a dry floor, a surge entering existing water or a reservoir draining | Select the initial downstream depth, the released gate or the open port. Follow the front and account for water crossing the boundary. |
| Rock in a river, river fork, two channels merging or an eddy near an inlet | Use the overhead field when flow can pass on either side. Compare downstream transport and obstacle exclusion with the same camera. |
| Pan, basin or vessel moving under player input | Apply its signed acceleration to the side-view water. Check both wall heights, their reversal and the closed volume. |
| Skill whose water charges, hits, splits, bursts or changes direction | Reuse the matching spell or compose a trigger sequence. Preserve the physical flight through impact, cancellation, recast and retirement. |

Each template has a declared geometry. Pressurized pipe networks, overturning ocean breakers, trapped air and interactions between immiscible fluids require additional solver models and their own native cases.
