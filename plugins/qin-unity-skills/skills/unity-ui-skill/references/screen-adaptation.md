# Screen adaptation

Choose a base composition and its reserved-region behavior from the [shared screen profiles](screen-profiles.md).

## Scale and geometry ownership

Use the game's approved design baseline in each supported orientation, including its declared 4K projection when applicable. Record visible and hit bounds, order, group centers, anchors, artwork sizes, offsets, gaps and text metrics in that baseline. Keep design pixels, layout units, active viewport pixels and capture dimensions distinct.

For portrait, normally stack major content groups and keep required actions within reach. For landscape, use the extra width for side-by-side regions or more grid columns while keeping the same content, priority and flow. Define the intended anchors and arrangement for both orientations when both are supported. Honor an explicit orientation lock and verify its runtime behavior.

After choosing the composition for the active orientation, default to one uniform multiplier `min(viewportWidth / referenceWidth, viewportHeight / referenceHeight)` on both axes. Apply it once to dimensions, hit areas, icon and text sizes, gaps and offsets. Account for the effective PanelSettings or CanvasScaler scale when converting to local units. Use the active viewport to calculate the scale and platform density to check touch ergonomics. Keep independent full-bleed backing in its own viewport layer. When the resulting layout misses readable type or hit-size minima, use an approved reflow, a compact layout or scrolling and verify that boundary. Preserve the aspect ratio of artwork and fixed-proportion controls in each variant. Use proportional cover for full-bleed art and proportional fit for an intact illustration. A scene camera follows the viewport it renders. A camera that supplies a character portrait or other fixed render texture follows that texture. When replaying viewport sizes, change the projection only on cameras that render the replayed viewport. Check the real projection and inspect a native frame before reducing its resolution for review.

For a screen-space Toolkit panel, use Scale With Screen Size and Expand when the entire design must fit. A screen-space Canvas can use Scale With Screen Size and Expand with the equivalent reference resolution. Read back the effective scale and geometry for the active host, including target textures and nested canvases. [Unity PanelScreenMatchMode](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/UIElements.PanelScreenMatchMode.html)

## UI Toolkit artwork and navigation

Locate the accepted PNG or Sprite and verify its source dimensions, trim, visible bounds and import settings. In UXML, give the hit target, backdrop, button faces, icons and caption separate elements. Apply USS `background-size: contain` to complete artwork and fit a single multiplier to its painted bounds. An artwork asset can retain transparent padding outside its visible bounds. Use proportional cover for full-screen scene backgrounds.

Use a centered flex row with equal-width slots and a maximum width for a fixed-proportion navigation plate. Wide windows can expose scene artwork beside the row. Change spacing or composition when available space changes and give localized captions their own width and baseline. Use nine-slice or tiling only for artwork with a reviewed, extensible center. Keep complete painted trims, icons, faces and progress rails proportional. Check actual paint, localized glyphs and native picking at the short, tall and wide profiles.

## Obstructions and orientation

Measure the real active display, window, safe area and available cutout or gesture bounds. Convert origin, axes and units through one existing layout owner. Treat safe-area insets and full viewport dimensions as different inputs. Compare each group's visible and hit bounds with relevant obstructions, keeping clear viewport-anchored corners or tool rows in their authored positions where the screen contract calls for them. Apply each inset once across nested parents. Recalculate from actual bounds after resize, rotation, display switch or inset change.

For a portrait iPhone with a centered notch or Dynamic Island, give the left wing, center and right wing separate layout owners within one semantic header. Keep the clear wings on a shared viewport-top line at their authored top and side margins. Allow them into the top inset when visible content and hit targets clear the cutout, rounded corners, status and gesture regions. Center the middle group below the obstruction plus clearance when the group would intersect it. If a wing lacks verified clearance or the obstruction expands, recalculate the affected group and use its safe-area fallback. With only a rectangular `Screen.safeArea`, keep essential center content below the top inset and require device evidence before treating each wing as clear. Check the full bounds of each group. In landscape, recalculate the affected side and bottom anchors for both supported rotation directions.

For a full-window top-left viewport, convert a bottom-left pixel rectangle using `top = viewportHeight - rect.yMax`. Then convert from pixels to the receiving panel units. Verify `Screen.cutouts` on the target Unity and iOS versions. Unity's iOS cutout bounds are device-table data, so use current platform obstruction evidence for expanded system overlays. [Unity safe area](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Screen-safeArea.html) · [Unity iOS display cutouts](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/iphone-API.html#display-cutout)

Confirm the active built-in iPhone Duo display before applying its layout. In outer upright portrait, end the top row at the left edge of the upper-right reservation. Align the existing controls to the physical lens center Y. Project that aperture from fixed upright space into each landscape turn. Start the top row to the right of an upper-left reservation. With a lower-right reservation, use the normal top row and keep nearby footer controls clear. Use proportional full-screen scenery. On inner display entry, discard the outer aperture and its cached offsets. Use the inner window’s current safe pane and select its portrait or landscape composition. [Duo layout and capture rules](screen-profiles.md#capture-each-iphone-duo-display-separately)

Keep separate evidence for requested physical devices, compatible simulator or emulator models, Editor replays of measured geometry and synthetic profiles. Record the source of measurements and the active display or fold pose. A profile name identifies a fixture and actual device geometry needs a runtime readback. On portrait-only projects verify the installed artifact remains upright when the device rotates. Include separate low and high pixel counts at the same aspect, valid different aspects, independently varied insets and the affected live transitions. Compare pixel rounding, anchor offsets, whole-world projection if relevant, hit geometry and frame composition in the same state.

## Painted bounds and readable content

Read the active font, fallback, outline and shadow and observe actual glyph ink within the rendered surface. Frame geometry, alpha bounds and the usable text region can differ. Check rounded edges, decorative icons, clipped content and label baselines. Project progress-track fill and overlay text into the painted track's own space, accounting for fit padding, trim and transparent borders. Compare empty, intermediate and full states. Verify equivalent groups across affected screens at matching inputs and give each intentional variant its declared slot or style.

Measure the usable size, complete joystick travel or other hit envelope, title and fixed actions, final scroll item and text readability. Check the longest supported translations and required content extremes in the actual host. Follow the same active flow through size, orientation or language changes where supported and verify state, focus, input and alignment settle after returning. Record screenshots and input outcomes with the actual runtime, revision and geometry.

Keep progress text in its own layout above the animated fill. Anchor both to the painted trough and fit the full glyphs, outline and shadow within its actual interior. If the resulting ink is too small, increase the source-proportional track or reflow its container. Inspect the affected live phases, including the longest visible value, and check clearance from the neighboring controls.

Apply the [locale acceptance matrix](layout-and-input.md#verify-each-locale) to the adapted layout. Measure text with its actual locale and font after uniform viewport fitting. Reserve room for longer translations at the minimum readable size and verify that any allowed local fitting still keeps content, neighboring controls and hit targets within their declared bounds.

Check grids and aligned peers at fractional scales. Leave space for rounding in a wrapping row or give the row an explicit column count. Compare settled edges and sizes in framebuffer pixels with a tolerance justified by the projection.
