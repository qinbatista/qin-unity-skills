# Staged effects

Bind the source ability or effect brief, trigger and intended result. Record the source requirements and the chosen visual design. Set the camera, subject scale and position, layer ordering, spatial anchors, and real scene budget before tuning individual effects. A world-space trail, camera-facing line, body surface and detached particles may require different spaces. Record each layer's job, anchor, sort order, stage window, nonlinear controls and expected capacity.

## Native lifecycle

Use the following phases when the receiving effect calls for the complete seven-stage lifecycle. Give every applicable phase a native entry, behavior, exit and observable state on one owning timeline. Keep gameplay state in its original owner so visual tails cannot extend a hit, shield or protection window.

| Phase | Visible contribution | Runtime boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare | Establish the origin and initial seed with zero or minimal coverage. | Acquire or reset the instance, bind anchors and establish the actual start condition. |
| Preparing | Gather, draw, grow or assemble partial forms. | Advance the nonlinear shape and separate contributions over their authored windows. |
| Prepared | Hold a readable charged state. | Wait for the real release gate after visible preparation is complete. |
| Releasing | Extend lines, open arcs, expand surfaces or scatter particles. | Start from the prepared state when the actual release is accepted. |
| Released | Show the active form, impact and any controlled hold. | Observe the gameplay owner's active window and any independent residual presentation. |
| Retracting | Unwind, reverse-draw, shrink, absorb or disperse. | Stop new work at the correct event and retract from the current values. |
| Recycled | Finish with zero coverage and no live tails or stale instance state. | Confirm completion, then reset and release or return the exact acquisition. |

Resource readiness can precede the cycle. Verify first visible preparation as well as warm playback. On interruption, retract the current partial shape and record which stages were reached. Preserve immediate owner destruction where it is required and distinguish it from natural completion. Exercise normal completion, early and late cancellation, disable and another use of the same pooled slot.

## Progressive formation and layers

- For lines, use an advancing path, extension from an origin or ordered assembly. Leave the unreached path absent and shape width, emission and accents separately.
- For rings, use an advancing arc, expanding or contracting radius, or a closing assembly. Define direction, closure and the initial extent. Inspect the first visible frame for a prematurely complete contour.
- For surfaces or volumes, move the reveal or dissolve front, deform, grow or assemble coverage in the source's actual shape. Coordinate the surface with line and particle progression. Verify a specified surface with its real material and gameplay-scale coverage.
- For particles, define gathering, emission spread, travel and terminal scatter or absorption. Move from local following to world space where detached particles need to stay behind. Check age response and actual bounded tail completion.

Test early, middle and late geometry separately from opacity. Trace path coverage, angle, radius or assembly in the native output. Use the `unity-animation-skill` motion-curve rules for the actual resulting time response of each independent control. Preserve the real clock and action events while shaping the visual progression.

For a layered complete effect, give the composition distinct independently controllable contributions. A surface or silhouette, directional energy and particulate accents provide three distinct roles for a profile that requires them. Add impact or residual layers as the brief and budget permit. Choose depth, occlusion, contrasting scale and foreground/background balance to keep the subject readable. Compare attribute variants in grayscale by silhouette, flow, start, main timing and finish. Verify bound body or world anchors throughout the action and preserve the underlying collision or hit behavior.
